Machine Learning Modeling & Dataset Methodology

The models driving this application were trained on 100,197 verified listings extracted from Ouedkniss. To guarantee realistic, leak-free evaluations, all transformations (Target Encoding, Scaling) were fitted inside training partitions under a 5-Fold Stratified Nested Cross-Validation strategy with Optuna hyperparameter optimization.
